THE GOAL
Work together to complete the Sacred Spiral journey. Each player is an Initiate exploring the magical forest. Draw cards, help your friends, and discover the mysteries of the Arcana.
YOUR TURN — four moments
1
PULSE — breathe & feel
Take a breath. Place your Activation Stone face-up. You are present.
2
STEP — move & draw
Move your piece 1–3 spaces on the board. Draw one card from the Arcana deck.
3
RITE — play a card
Play one card from your hand. Read it aloud and do what it says. You may help a friend instead of yourself.
4
SEAL — complete & log
Log any Echo artifacts you gained. Turn your Stone dim. Pass the sacred flame.
YOUR FOREST WORLD
⬡ Echo Artifacts are the magical objects you collect on your journey.
○ Seeds are challenges — each one you overcome helps you grow.
✦ Forest Totems mark your journey stages: Seedling → Sapling → Grove-Keeper → Elder.
∆ The Frame card is always in the centre — it watches over everyone.
