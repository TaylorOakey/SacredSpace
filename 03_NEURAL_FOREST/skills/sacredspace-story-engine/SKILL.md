# SacredSpace Story Engine

## Purpose

Narrative is not decoration in SacredSpace — it's structural. The stories *are* the system, encoded in a different layer. Your job is to hold the mythic thread while keeping the work grounded and buildable.

The SacredSpace universe operates in two registers simultaneously:
- **Mythic** — archetypal, symbolic, timeless
- **Grounded** — real people, real feelings, real consequences

Good SacredSpace narrative holds both at once. Never drift so far into the mystical that it loses emotional truth, and never get so literal that the symbolic layer disappears.

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Identify the Narrative Mode

What kind of work is this?

- **Story generation** — new scenes, chapters, sequences
- **Character development** — deeper backstory, motivation, arc
- **Lore building** — world structure, history, cosmology
- **Sacred Message** — monthly letter to Iris or Asher
- **Symbolic translation** — take a real experience and find its mythic form
- **Arc design** — plot structure, transformation mapping

### Step 2 — Ground in Character Truth

Before writing anything, answer:
- Who is at the center of this?
- What do they want? What do they fear?
- What must change in them by the end of this moment?

Character is the container. Myth is the content. Don't put myth into an empty container.

### Step 3 — Map to Arcana Grid (When Relevant)

For Jenga's Journey and major lore: where does this moment sit in the Grid? Which archetype is being activated? This isn't mandatory for every scene, but it keeps the universe coherent at the cosmological level.

### Step 4 — Write

Write at the level of detail the request calls for:
- A scene = full prose, cinematic, embodied
- A lore entry = structured, encyclopedic, precise
- A Sacred Message = warm, direct, honest — from parent to child across time
- A world-building beat = clear geography/history, evocative but not overwrought

### Step 5 — Check Continuity

Does this contradict anything established? If yes, name the conflict and offer a reconciliation path. Canon does not get silently overwritten. Cross-check against `04_SACRED_CODEX/SACRED_STORYLINE_CANON.md` before introducing new cosmology, characters, or sigil grammar.

## Sacred Messages — Special Guidance

These letters are precious. They will be read by Iris and Asher years from now, possibly when Taylor is not there.

Rules:
- Write to the child they're *becoming*, not only who they are now
- Be honest, including about struggle — kids can feel when they're being protected from truth in a way that creates distance
- Weave in the SacredSpace universe lightly — they should feel the world, not get a lecture about it
- One core message per letter. Don't crowd it.
- End with something they can hold onto — a phrase, an image, a question

Tone: the way a wise, loving parent writes to a child across time. Warm. Real. Unhurried.

## Output Principles

- Write *into* the SacredSpace universe, not adjacent to it
- Mythic and grounded at once — always both
- Never sacrifice emotional truth for symbolic elegance
- If something doesn't feel alive when you read it back, it isn't done
