The Magician
Fire · Initiation · Node 01 of 12
The Magician stands at the threshold between intention and form. Not yet the Alchemist who transmutes — the Magician first names the flame. In the grammar of the Arcana, Initiation is always an act of naming: what is called forth must first be seen.
"What begins as spark becomes doctrine. The Magician does not perform power — the Magician is the permission."
In Lakesh — Alakin.
