✦  Transmission
The Magician stands at the threshold between intention and form. Not yet the Alchemist who transmutes — the Magician first names the flame.
In the grammar of the Arcana, Initiation is always an act of naming: what is called forth must first be seen. The Magician does not perform power — the Magician is the permission. This is the distinction that divides the practitioner from the performer.
Fire at Initiation does not burn — it illuminates. The Magician's gift is not destruction or transformation, but recognition: the capacity to perceive the sacred name of a thing and speak it aloud into the world. From this act, all other rites proceed.
