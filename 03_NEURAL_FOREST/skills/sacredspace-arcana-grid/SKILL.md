# SacredSpace Arcana Grid

## The Law
Metatron-as-Law holds the center. The Grid is not decorative — it is the structural physics of the SacredSpace universe.

## Structure: 4 Elements × 3 Primes = 12 Archetypes

| Element | Prime 1 | Prime 2 | Prime 3 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Fire    | The Spark | The Forge | The Beacon |
| Water   | The Deep  | The Current | The Mirror |
| Earth   | The Root  | The Builder | The Keeper |
| Air     | The Breath | The Messenger | The Seer |

## Canon Rules
- Grid is canon-locked. Do not modify without Taylor's explicit revision.
- Metatron-as-Law = the organizing principle, not an archetype.
- Each archetype maps to an agent, a pillar, or a creative function.
- When generating SacredSpace lore, characters, or systems — anchor to the Grid first.
- If the canonical archetype names differ from the above, ask Taylor for the locked version rather than guessing.
