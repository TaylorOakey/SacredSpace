# **STEP 1. Install Obsidian and Create Your Vault**
(You may have already done this, but here’s the quick ceremony.)

1. Open the Obsidian app.  
2. Tap **Create New Vault**.  
3. Name it: **SACREDSPACE : MASTER REALM**  
4. Choose a folder on your device where this vault will live.

That’s it. Your “vault” is just a folder that Obsidian reads.

---

# **STEP 2. Create Your 7-Pillar Root Structure**
Inside your vault, create **7 folders**.  
Obsidian works best when you keep it minimal.

Here are the recommended Pillars for your SacredSpace OS:

1. **01_LORE & STORY**  
2. **02_GAME SYSTEMS**  
3. **03_OS ARCHITECTURE** (NotebookLM, Bodhi, Codex, Drive Mirror)  
4. **04_PROJECTS: EARTHSIDE** (Nursery, jobs, house, car logs, etc.)  
5. **05_FAMILY REALMS** (Asher Realm, Iris Realm, Jeanie’s Circle)  
6. **06_RITUALS & GROWTH** (journal, meditation notes, routines)  
7. **07_ARCHIVES** (old imports, unorganized notes, temp uploads)

Nothing fancy.  
Just seven doors.

Your mind instantly knows where things belong.

# **STEP 4. Turn on ONLY the Essential Plugins**
Do not drown in plugins.  
Here are the 4 you *actually* need right now.

Turn these on under “Community Plugins”:

1. **Templater**  
Lets us create sacred templates for everything (characters, rituals, tasks).

2. **Dataview**  
Lets your vault become an automatic dashboard.

3. **Periodic Notes**  
Daily, weekly, monthly journals that become a rhythm.

4. **Canvas** (built-in)  
Perfect for mapping city designs, family OS, game boards.

Everything else can wait.

Your mind needs simplicity.

# **STEP 6. Connect Obsidian to Your Google Drive Mirror**
Since your OS lives across chats, Drive, and NotebookLM, we create a ritual for syncing.

The easiest path:

1. Install **Drive for Desktop** on your phone or computer.  
2. Choose a folder inside your Drive called:  
   **SACREDSPACE_MIRROR**  
3. Put your Obsidian Vault inside that folder  
   OR  
4. Use a sync plugin like “Google Drive Sync” (only if needed).

This creates the “obsidian mirror” you asked for.

NotebookLM reads your notes.  
Obsidian reads your notes.  
ChatGPT exports into Drive.  
Drive reflects into Obsidian.

A triangle of clarity.
