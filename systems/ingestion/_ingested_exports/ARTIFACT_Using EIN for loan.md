## Step 2: Build a baseline of business credit and financial credibility

Using an EIN is powerful, but most lenders will look at more than just your EIN. They’ll want assurance your business is creditworthy.  

1. **Apply for a business credit card or small line of credit**  
   - With your EIN, open a small business credit card or vendor line of credit.  
   - Always make payments on time (or early).  

2. **Establish vendor/supplier accounts that report to credit bureaus**  
   - Work with vendors who report your payments to Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business, or Equifax Business.  
   - Paying trade accounts on time builds your business credit profile.  

3. **Keep clean, consistent bookkeeping & financial statements**  
   - Maintain good accounting records: profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow.  
   - Use accounting software so your financials are auditable.  

4. **Monitor your business credit reports**  
   - Regularly check your business credit scores from Dun & Bradstreet, Experian, and Equifax.  
   - Dispute any errors early.

5. **Demonstrate revenue, cash flow, or contracts**  
   - Even if you haven’t launched fully, show pre-sales, contracts, subscriptions, or commitments to show you have demand.  
   - Lenders like evidence of incoming cash flow or future business.
