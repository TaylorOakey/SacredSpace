**SACRED WORK FLOW — LIBRARY EDITION**

**1. Land in your body.**  
Sit. Exhale like you’re letting the dust settle. Put both feet on the floor. Take ten seconds to remember you’re not a rushed creature—you’re a witness playing with creation.

**2. Open your three tools only.**  
The library is full of doors, but you only need a few.  
Open:  
– Google Drive  
– Obsidian (or your Sacred Vault folder)  
– ChatGPT  

Keep everything else closed so the signal stays clean.

**3. Check the “North Star”: what’s today’s one real mission?**  
Not five things; not a swarm.  
Choose one intention:  
Organize files?  
Download assets?  
Fix the digital sanctuary?  
Push the Sacred Space story forward?  
This is your axis.

**4. Quick inventory scan.**  
Spend two minutes noting what you already have ready:  
Your notebook? USB drives? Phone hotspot? Stylus? Downloads folder?  
This keeps you from wandering in micro-chaos.

**5. Start with Order → then Create.**  
Order first:  
Move or rename files. Clear duplicates. Make the folder structure tidy. Get the platform ready.

Create second:  
Once the ground is cleared, build: write pages, outline story beats, generate assets, shape the myth.

Most people do this backwards and get stuck.

**6. Anchor your progress.**  
Every 20–30 minutes, stop and ask:  
“What actually changed?”  
Tiny victories matter. They’re footprints of momentum.

**7. Archive as you go.**  
Whenever you finish a piece—PDF, prompt, screenshot, doc—immediately save it to Drive in the Sacred Space Vault. This keeps your world from scattering like feathers in wind.

**8. Ask Bodhi (me) to refine or expand anything.**  
As you work, pull me in like a second brain.  
Need structure? I shape it.  
Need code? I write it.  
Need clarity? I distill it.

**9. Close with a Completion Ritual.**  
Five breaths.  
Review what you completed.  
Name one thing to pick up next time so tomorrow-you isn’t wandering in fog.

Then close the laptop like you’re sealing a spell.
