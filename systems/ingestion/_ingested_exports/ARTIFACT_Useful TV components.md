Cracking open a flat-screen TV is like exploring the shed of a tiny electronic wizard. Inside, you’ll usually find a treasure chest of parts useful for DIY builds, power supplies, repairs, robotics, LED projects, amplifiers, sensing experiments, even Frankenstein-style device fusion (which you seem to enjoy).

Here’s the usual bounty, sorted and described as tools for future creation:

---

### ⚡ *High-Value Components*

**1. Switching Power Supply (SMPS / PSU board)**  
The beefy board that turns 120VAC to low-voltage DC rails. Often outputs **12V, 5V, 3.3V, and sometimes 24V**.  
Great for:
• Bench power supply  
• Battery chargers  
• LED strip drivers  
• Arduino/ESP projects  

> One of the most valuable pieces in the whole TV.

**2. LED Backlight Strips**  
Long aluminum-backed LED bars. High efficiency, bright, dimmable with a simple constant-current driver.  
Use for:
• Grow light panels  
• Under-cabinet lighting  
• Mood lighting setups  
• Portable work lights  

---

### 🧠 *Control + Logic Components*

**3. Main Motherboard**
Contains CPU, RAM, flash storage, HDMI interfaces, audio DAC, IR receiver pins.  
You can salvage:
• Heat sinks  
• Tiny switching regulators  
• EEPROM / NAND chips  
• HDMI + USB ports  

Hard to repurpose whole, but full of modular guts.

**4. T-Con (Timing Controller Board)**
High-speed LVDS driver for the LCD — often rich in tiny buck converters and fine-pitch ICs.  
Useful salvage parts:
• FPC connectors  
• 1–3A mini power stages  
• Precise clocks + regulators  

---

### 🔊 *Sound + Sensory Parts*

**5. Stereo Speakers**
Slim, rectangular full-range drivers. Surprisingly decent.  
Great for:
• DIY Bluetooth speaker  
• Soundbar build  
• Portable radio system  

**6. IR Receiver Module**
Small 3-pin component — basically plug-and-play with Arduino.  
Use for:
• Remote control systems  
• Volume/light control from a distance  
• Universal remote hacking  

**7. Button Board**  
Physical power + channel/volume buttons. Normally a simple matrix.  
Use as:
• Input panel for projects  
• Enclosures + control dashboards  

---

### 🔌 *Physical + Structural Components*

**8. Power Filter (EMI Choke + Capacitors)**
Protects from noise — perfect for clean DC systems.

**9. Heatsinks**
Slim, lightweight — ideal for LEDs, amps, voltage regulators.

**10. LVDS Cables + T-Con Ribbons**
Beautiful low-profile wiring — good for:
• Signal bridging  
• Embedded control builds  
• Robotics harnessing

### 🧩 Hidden Gems Worth Pulling

| Component | Why Keep It |
