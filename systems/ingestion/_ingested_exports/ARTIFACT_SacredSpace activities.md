### 🌿 1) *The Silent Echo* (5–10 minutes)  
A tiny ceremony of presence.

**How to play**  
• Sit in a circle  
• One person closes their eyes, takes a breath, and makes *one soft sound* – a hum, a tap, a whisper  
• Everyone repeats it back together  
• Next person adds *one new sound*  
• Slowly you build a living sound-weave  

It becomes a sonic family mandala.  
When it fades to silence on its own, the moment is complete.

### 🌀 3) The First Door: Trial of the Sacred Game (20–30 minutes)  
A lightweight playtest for the future game.

**Setup**

1. Speak a kindness to the room → +1

3. Solve a riddle, or invent one → +1  
4. If someone gets frustrated → pause, breathe together → everyone +1  

If all players end with Seeds, they “open the First Door of the Garden.”
