GoFundMe is a **crowdfunding platform** where people raise money for personal, creative, or charitable causes by sharing their story online and receiving donations from friends, family, and strangers.  

Here’s the breakdown of **how it works**:  

---

### **1. Create a Campaign**
- **Sign up** (free) and create a fundraising page.
- You write a **title**, tell your **story** (why you’re raising money, who it’s for, and how it will be used), and set a **goal amount**.
- You can add **photos, videos, and updates** to make it more personal and trustworthy.

---

### **2. Share Your Campaign**
- GoFundMe works best when shared **directly** — via text, email, and especially social media (Facebook, Instagram, etc.).
- They give you a **share link** you can post anywhere.
- Most successful fundraisers get their first donations from **people they already know**, and those people sharing it helps it reach strangers.

---

### **3. Receive Donations**
- Anyone can donate using a **credit card, debit card, PayPal, or Apple Pay**.
- Donors can choose to **tip GoFundMe** (instead of GoFundMe taking a percentage from you, they mostly rely on tips from donors).
- You can see who donated (unless they chose to remain anonymous).

---

### **4. Withdraw Funds**
- Funds are available as soon as donations start coming in.
- You connect your bank account to withdraw money — either **as a lump sum or ongoing**.
- It can take a couple of business days for funds to hit your bank.
- You don’t have to meet your goal to get the money — **you keep whatever you raise**.

---

### **5. Fees**
- **Platform Fee:** $0 (GoFundMe itself is free to use — in most countries).
- **Transaction Fee:** Around **2.9% + $0.30 per donation** (covers payment processing).
- Donors can add a tip for GoFundMe, but you still receive the full donation minus the transaction fee.

---

### **6. Ongoing Updates**
- Campaign creators can post **updates** to keep donors informed, thank them, and encourage more sharing.
- Consistent updates help maintain trust and increase donations.

---

✅ **Best For**  
- Emergencies (medical, disaster relief)
- Memorials/funerals  
- Education or creative projects  
- Community causes  
- Nonprofit fundraising  

⚠️ **Not Ideal For**  
- Anonymous, unverified causes (people tend to be skeptical)  
- Causes without a clear story or urgency
