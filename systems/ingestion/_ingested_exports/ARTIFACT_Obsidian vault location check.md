### How to know where your vault actually lives
Obsidian stores each vault as a **regular folder** on your computer. The trick is simply figuring out *which* folder Obsidian is looking at.

Do this on your computer:

1. Open **Obsidian**.  
2. Look at the **left sidebar** → click the **vault name**.  
3. Choose **“Open folder in system explorer”** (Windows Explorer).  
4. This will open the exact folder Obsidian is using.

Now ask yourself:  
Is the folder path something like:

```
C:\Users\<yourname>\Google Drive\Digital sacred sanctuary
```

or

```
/Users/<yourname>/Google Drive/SACREDSPACE/
```

If yes → your Obsidian vault is indeed *living inside Google Drive*.  
If no → it’s stored locally on the computer.

### Why this matters
If it's **not** inside Google Drive, then:
- It won’t sync automatically.
- NotebookLM and Google AI Studio won’t see it.
- Your phone won’t see it unless you use Syncthing or manually sync.
