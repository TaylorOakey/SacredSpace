### **F.A.C.T.S. Communication Framework (Common Interpretation)**

| Letter | Stands For           | Purpose                                                                 |
|--------|----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **F**  | **Feelings**         | Express your honest emotions without blame. (“I feel…”)                |
| **A**  | **Actions**          | Describe specific behaviors or events neutrally. (“When you…”)         |
| **C**  | **Consequences**     | Share how those actions affect you or others. (“It results in…”)       |
| **T**  | **Thoughts**         | Explain your perspective or beliefs. (“I think…”)                       |
| **S**  | **Solutions**        | Propose or ask for a positive path forward. (“I suggest… / Can we…?”)  |

---

### 🧠 **Why It’s Used**
- Promotes **nonviolent communication**
- Builds **emotional intelligence**
- Encourages **accountability without shame or blame**
- Helps youth and adults alike develop healthy ways to express themselves

> “I **feel** frustrated when you interrupt me while I’m speaking (**action**), because it makes me feel unheard (**consequence**). I **think** we both have important things to say, and I want to hear yours too. **Can we** agree to let each other finish our thoughts first?” (**solution**)
