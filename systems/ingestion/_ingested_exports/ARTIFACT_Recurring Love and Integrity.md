## 🔧 SETUP OPTIONS (Pick 1 or combine)
Depending on your tech preference and what platforms your kids will use as they grow older:

### **Option A: Email Automation (most flexible & futureproof)**
Best if they have or will have email addresses.

**Tools Needed:**
- Gmail account (yours)
- Free tool like [Mailmeteor](https://mailmeteor.com), [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com), or [Zapier](https://zapier.com)

**Steps:**
1. **Create Gmail Contacts for Each Child** with their email address.
2. **Write 60 messages** (1 per month for 5 years) in a Google Sheet:
   - Column A: Date
   - Column B: Subject (e.g., “August Message of Love ❤️”)
   - Column C: Message body (include checklist here)
3. **Use Mailmeteor or Zapier** to automate sending each message from the sheet on the correct date.

---

### **Option B: Shared Calendar Reminders**
If they have access to Google Calendar on tablets/phones.

**Steps:**
1. Create a new Google Calendar called “Love Notes from Dad.”
2. Add **monthly recurring events** with titles like “Check Your Heart 💖” or “You Are So Loved.”
3. In the event notes, write:
   - ✅ Did I tell the truth this month?
   - ✅ Did I help someone who didn’t ask?
   - ✅ Did I take care of myself?
   - ❤️ “Remember, I love you no matter what.”

---

### **Option C: Physical Binder + Digital Backup**
If you want them to have something tangible and digital.

1. Create a **Sacred Monthly Integrity Journal** (PDF).
2. Print out a copy to give them yearly.
3. Upload digital copies to a shared Google Drive folder.
4. Set monthly text reminders from your phone with links to the journal.
