Firefox for Android *does* support extensions.

### Steps:
1. Open **Google Play Store**  
2. Install **Firefox for Android**  
3. Open Firefox and go to:  
   `https://addons.mozilla.org`
4. Search for **EZ Folders**

6. Log into ChatGPT from Firefox  
7. Now the little EZFolders panel should appear or be accessible from the browser’s extension menu

- Tap the **three dots** in Firefox  
- Look under **Add-ons**  
- Tap **EZFolders** to open its interface  
- Then refresh ChatGPT
