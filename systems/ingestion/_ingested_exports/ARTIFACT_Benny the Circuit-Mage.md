**Valient Thorr** is an American [hard rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_rock)/[heavy metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_metal_music) band from [Greenville, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenville,_North_Carolina).[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valient_Thorr#cite_note-1) The name of the band is based on the [urban legend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_legend) of Valiant Thor, an Alien which came from [Venus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus) to Earth. Valient explained, "That'll be the background for the band! We're from space ..."[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valient_Thorr#cite_note-2)
