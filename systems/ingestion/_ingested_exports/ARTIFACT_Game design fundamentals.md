## **1. Core Fundamentals of a Card/Board Game**
Before you think about the art, you need the *skeleton* — the structure that keeps the game engaging and balanced.

### **A. Core Concept & Theme**
- **Theme:** This is the “soul” of the game. *Sacred Space* already has a strong identity — spiritual journey, nature, hero’s path, balance of city and tribal wisdom.
- **Tone:** Decide if it’s cooperative (players work together), competitive, or a hybrid.
- **Narrative Hook:** Each game session should feel like a chapter in a larger story.

💡 *Sacred Space suggestion:* Have the main storyline mirror the **Hero’s Journey**, where each player’s growth path is reflected by their progression on the board.

---

### **B. Victory Conditions**
- **Clear Goal:** How do players win? Do they collect sacred tokens? Reach the final “Awakening” space? Achieve a personal destiny before others?
- **Multiple Paths to Victory:** This increases replay value — for example, a player could win by either completing spiritual quests or gathering enough nature-energy to heal the Sacred Tree.

💡 *Sacred Space suggestion:* Include 2–3 victory paths:  
1. **Awakening Path** (complete your destiny quests)  
2. **Healing Path** (restore Sacred Spaces on the board)  
3. **Balance Path** (collect and align city and nature energy in harmony)

---

### **C. Components**
Most card–board games are a mix of these:
- **Game Board**: A physical map or sacred diagram.
- **Cards**: Events, quests, blessings, obstacles.
- **Tokens/Resources**: Represent energy, health, items, or allies.
- **Character Pieces**: Miniatures, standees, or tokens that represent each player.
- **Dice/Spinners**: For randomness, if desired.
- **Player Boards**: Personal mats to track stats, inventory, or abilities.

💡 *Sacred Space suggestion:*  
- **Board**: A circular mandala design, divided into different zones (City, Jungle, Sacred Sites, Shadow Realm).  
- **Cards**: Color-coded decks for Blessings, Challenges, Spirit Guides, and City Encounters.  
- **Tokens**: Smooth wooden discs or crystal-like pieces to represent life-force energy.  
- **Character Pieces**: Mini standees or flat illustrated tokens of the characters (Jenga, shaman girl, etc.).

---

### **D. Flow of the Game**
A great game has a rhythm — tension builds, climaxes, and resolves.

**Typical turn sequence:**
1. **Draw Phase** – Player draws a card from a relevant deck.  
2. **Action Phase** – Player moves, interacts with the board, trades, or uses abilities.  
3. **Resolution Phase** – Effects happen (gain/lose energy, advance quests, trigger enemies).

💡 *Sacred Space improvement:*  
Make turns feel like *story beats*. Instead of "Draw–Move–Act," call them:
1. **Receive Guidance** (draw card)  
2. **Take the Journey** (move or act)  
3. **Face the Consequence** (resolve effects)

---

### **E. Balance & Replayability**
- **Test with Different Player Counts:** The game should be fun with 2 players *and* 6 players.  
- **Scalability:** Board events and challenge levels should adjust to the number of players.  
- **Randomization:** Shuffle board tiles, change starting locations, or rotate victory paths to keep each game fresh.

💡 *Sacred Space suggestion:*  
Make some Sacred Sites interchangeable tiles — they can be placed differently each game so the path to victory changes.
