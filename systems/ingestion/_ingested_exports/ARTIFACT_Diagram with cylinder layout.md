## 2️⃣ Common Causes for 4 & 5 Misfire Together
If two adjacent cylinders misfire, it’s often something **shared between them** rather than two totally separate failures.

### 🔹 Ignition-related
- **Bad coil pack** — Coils can fail in a way that affects two cylinders if there’s a wiring issue or shared power/ground.
- **Damaged spark plugs** — If old or fouled, replace them. Check plug gap and condition.
- **Wiring harness damage** — The coil connectors for 4 & 5 run through the same branch of the harness.

### 🔹 Fuel-related
- **Clogged or dead fuel injectors** on both cylinders.
- **Shared fuel rail issue** — Unlikely but possible if contaminants are clogging part of the rail.

### 🔹 Air / mechanical issues
- **Vacuum leak** near those runners in the intake manifold (Bank 2 side leak will affect 4 & 5 more).
- **Low compression** — Blown head gasket between the two cylinders, burnt valves, or cracked head will cause adjacent cylinders to misfire.

---

## 3️⃣ Step-by-Step Diagnosis
Here’s how you can test without wasting money:

1. **Swap coils**  
   Move coil from 4 to 1 and coil from 5 to 2.  
   - If misfire moves to new cylinders → coil(s) bad.  
   - If misfire stays on 4 & 5 → look elsewhere.

2. **Inspect spark plugs**  
   - Remove plugs 4 & 5 and check for oil, coolant, carbon fouling, or physical damage.
   - Replace if worn (NGK Laser Platinum recommended).

3. **Check injector operation**  
   - Listen with a mechanic’s stethoscope or screwdriver — you should hear a clicking sound.
   - If silent, test resistance (~11–13 Ω) or swap injectors between cylinders to see if misfire follows.

4. **Vacuum leak check**  
   - Spray carb cleaner around intake manifold gaskets near cylinders 4 & 5 — if RPM changes, you’ve got a leak.

5. **Compression or leak-down test**  
   - If ignition and fuel check out, verify cylinder compression.  
     Spec: ~185 psi; below 135 psi means trouble.

---

## 4️⃣ Quick Note About Multiple Misfires on Bank 2
Because your **VTEC solenoid assembly and oil pressure to Bank 2** play a role in valve timing, a bad solenoid or oil pressure issue could cause misfires **only on Bank 2** (cylinders 4–6).  
If you also have **P2647/P1259 codes** or low oil pressure, this might be related.
