### ⚙ **ASSISTANT** — 2025-11-25 23:58

When a laptop has taken a fall and the screen won’t wake, the trouble usually hides in one of three realms:  
• the **display connection is loose**,  
• the **internal components aren’t getting power**, or  
• the **hardware itself is damaged and needs a deeper fix**.

Let’s go step-by-step, like working through a dark forest with a lantern and patience.

---

### 🌿 Quick Checks (the easy wins)  
Try these first:

1. **Hold power for 30 seconds**  
   This drains residual charge and can unstuck a frozen board.  
   Then power back on normally.

2. **Check charging brick + port**  
   • Plug in and leave for 10 minutes  
   • Look for charging LED  
   • Try a different charger if possible  
   If no lights come on, we may have power-rail damage.

3. **External display test**  
   You’ll need a TV/monitor + HDMI cable.  
   • Plug laptop → TV  
   • Turn both on  
   • Press `Fn + F4` (or similar display switch key)  
   If it shows video externally, your internal panel or ribbon cable likely popped loose.

---

### 🌿 If screen stays black, we move deeper

#### 1. **Hard Reboot**
- Unplug charger  
- Hold power button 1 full minute  
- Try powering on again

#### 2. **Check for signs of life**
Tell me what you notice:  
- Do keyboard lights flash?  
- Do fans spin?  
- Does the power light turn on at all?  
Each one is a clue.

---

### 🌿 If you’re comfortable opening it (optional but helpful)

Sometimes a fall jolts the **display ribbon cable** out of place.

If you’re willing, I can guide you step by step:

1. Remove back cover screws  
2. Gently pop off bottom shell  
3. Locate the **display ribbon near the hinge**  
4. Press connector firmly into place  
5. Reassemble → test power again

This is one of the most common fixes after a drop.

Most HP Streams come with soldered 32–64GB eMMC storage.

💡 *Workaround:* Install ChromeOS Flex or lightweight Linux to run off USB/SSD externally.
