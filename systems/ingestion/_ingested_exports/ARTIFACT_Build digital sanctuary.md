## PHASE 1 – Open the Gate (Log in & Create the Sanctuary Folder)

1. **Log into Google**
   - On the library computer, open Chrome (or any browser).
   - Go to Google → sign into your main account.

2. **Open Google Drive**
   - Click the 9-dot grid (Google Apps) → **Drive**.

3. **Create the main Sanctuary folder**
   - In Drive, click **+ New → Folder**.
   - Name it:  
     `A SACREDSPACE – Digital Sanctuary`
   - Open that folder. Everything below happens *inside* this.

## PHASE 3 – Create the Heart: The MASTER INDEX Doc

Now we make the “brain” of the sanctuary.

1. **Create the doc**
   - Go into `0_MASTER_INDEX`.
   - Click **+ New → Google Doc**.
   - Name it:  
     `SACREDSPACE – Master Index`

2. **Add top-level sections**

   Paste/type something like this at the top:

   ```text
   SACREDSPACE – MASTER INDEX

   1. Dashboard / Today
   2. Lore & Story
   3. Game & Systems
   4. Art & Media
   5. Business & Grants
   6. Rituals & Journals
   7. Money & POD
   8. Archive / Inbox
   ```

3. **Turn each into a heading**
   - Highlight each line (`1. Dashboard / Today`, etc.).
   - At the top toolbar, change from “Normal text” → **Heading 2** (or Heading 1).
   - This lets Google Docs auto-build a navigation panel.

4. **Link to folders from the index**
   - Under each heading, add a bullet like:
     - `Folder: 1_LORE & STORY`
   - To link it:
     - Go to Drive tab → right-click the folder → **Get link** → **Copy link**.
     - Back in the Doc, highlight the text `1_LORE & STORY` → **Insert → Link** → paste.

   Do this for:
   - Lore & Story → `1_LORE & STORY`
   - Game & Systems → `2_GAME & SYSTEMS`
   - Art & Media → `3_ART & MEDIA`
   - Business & Grants → `4_BUSINESS & GRANTS`
   - Rituals & Journals → `5_RITUALS & JOURNALS`
   - Money & POD → `6_MONEY & POD`
   - Archive / Inbox → `7_INBOX (UNSORTED)`

Now this single doc is your **portal** to everything.
