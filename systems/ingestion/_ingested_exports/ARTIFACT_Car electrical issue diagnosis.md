---

### 🔍 **Dashboard Warning Lights in Your Image:**
1. **Battery light (🔋)** – Indicates a charging system issue (alternator, battery, wiring).
2. **TCS (Traction Control System) & ABS lights** – Often triggered by low voltage.
3. **Brake warning light** – Could be tied to ABS or just due to voltage drop.
4. **CEL (Check Engine Light)** – Likely triggered when systems started failing.
5. **Airbag light** – Also can be triggered by low voltage or electrical problems.

---

### 🚨 **What Likely Happened:**
- The **alternator stopped charging** the battery (due to failure, belt slip/break, or connection issue).
- Once the battery voltage dropped too low, **the ECU, sensors, and gauges** started to fail (speedometer stopped).
- Eventually, the **car lost ignition power** and shut off completely.

---

### ✅ **What to Check First:**
1. **Battery voltage** (engine off and engine running):
   - Should be ~12.4–12.7V with engine off.
   - Should be ~13.5–14.5V with engine running.
   - If running voltage is low or drops, **alternator isn't charging**.

2. **Alternator belt** – Make sure it's not broken or slipping.

3. **Battery terminals and ground wire** – Check for corrosion or loose connections.

4. **OBD2 codes** – Scan the vehicle to confirm if a **P0562 (low voltage)** or similar code is present.

---

### 🛠️ Recommended Next Steps:
- **Test or replace the alternator** if it's not charging.
- If alternator is fine, have the **battery load tested**.
- Check for any **blown fuses or fusible links** in the engine bay fuse box.
