Because you’re on Android, here’s how to delete chats in the ChatGPT app:

### How to delete a single chat
1. Open the ChatGPT app.
2. Tap the **menu icon (≡)** in the top-left to open your conversation history.
3. Find the chat you want to remove.
4. **Press and hold** the conversation title.
5. In the popup, tap **Delete** (in red), then confirm. citeturn0search0turn0search2  

### How to quickly clean a bunch of clutter
Here’s a simple filter so you don’t wipe something important:

Keep:
- Any chat that:
  - Feeds **SacredSpace** worldbuilding, OS design, character forges, or game rules  
  - Contains **practical info** you’ll reuse (job search plans, invoices, repair steps, school info)
  - Feels emotionally important (letters to kids, spiritual insights you want to revisit)

Safe to delete / archive:
- “Test” chats: “hi,” “does this work,” random one-liners
- Duplicate brainstorms where a later chat has the **more complete version**
- One-off questions you’ll never need again (like “what do elves do” if we already baked that into lore)

If you’d rather *hide* clutter without deleting it forever, you can also **archive** instead of delete:

1. Open the chat history sidebar.
2. Tap & hold a chat, or tap the **three dots (⋯)** beside it.
3. Tap **Archive**. citeturn0search1  

Archived chats vanish from your main list but can still be accessed & unarchived later.
