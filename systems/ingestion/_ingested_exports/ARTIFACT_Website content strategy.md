## 3. Website Architecture & Navigation

**Primary Navigation Menu:**
- **Home** – Brand overview, featured art/products, intro to graphic novel.  
- **Blog** – Articles on creativity, sacred living, art features, behind-the-scenes.  
- **Graphic Novel** – Serialized issues, character bios, lore archive.  
- **Art Compendium** – Gallery of visual works (your art + guest artists).  
- **Digital Store** – Shop prints, books, game assets, music, apparel, sacred objects.  
- **About Us** – Mission, vision, team/creator story.  
- **Community** – Forum, newsletter signup, events & workshops.  
- **Contact** – Connect for commissions, collaborations, support.  

**Internal Link Flow:**  
- Blog articles → related store products + graphic novel references.  
- Graphic novel chapters → lore entries in the art compendium + shop tie-ins.  
- Art compendium → artist spotlights → link to blog + store.  
- Store products → linked blog posts + compendium inspiration pages.
