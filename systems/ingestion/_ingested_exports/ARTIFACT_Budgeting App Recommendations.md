### 🔝 **Top Budgeting Apps for 2025**

#### 1. **You Need a Budget (YNAB)**
- **Best for**: Proactive budgeting and habit building.
- **Features**:
  - Assign every dollar a job (zero-based budgeting).
  - Syncs with your bank accounts.
  - Tracks rent, gas, groceries, etc. in categories.
  - Goal setting and debt payoff tracking.
- **Platforms**: Android, iOS, Web.
- **Cost**: Free 34-day trial, then ~$14.99/month or $99/year.

---

#### 2. **Goodbudget**
- **Best for**: Envelope-style budgeting.
- **Features**:
  - Manually track expenses in envelopes (rent, gas, groceries, etc.).
  - Great for couples/families to budget together.
  - Simple, ad-free design.
- **Platforms**: Android, iOS, Web.
- **Cost**: Free version or $8/month (or $70/year) for unlimited envelopes.

---

#### 3. **EveryDollar**
- **Best for**: Simple zero-based budgeting (from Dave Ramsey).
- **Features**:
  - Easy to use for tracking monthly categories.
  - Manual entry or connect bank (with paid plan).
  - Helps create a monthly plan with priorities like rent, gas, groceries.
- **Platforms**: Android, iOS, Web.
- **Cost**: Free version available; premium ~$12.99/month.

---

#### 4. **PocketGuard**
- **Best for**: Automatically showing how much you can safely spend.
- **Features**:
  - Links to your bank and cards.
  - Automatically tracks bills, subscriptions, and everyday expenses.
  - "In My Pocket" feature shows how much is safe to spend.
- **Platforms**: Android, iOS.
- **Cost**: Free basic plan, ~$7.99/month for premium.

---

#### 5. **Spendee**
- **Best for**: Visual spend tracking with shared wallets.
- **Features**:
  - Beautiful, colorful visual interface.
  - Track different wallets (personal, shared, cash).
  - Good for budgeting across categories like rent, utilities, groceries, etc.
- **Platforms**: Android, iOS, Web.
- **Cost**: Free version available, ~$2.99/month premium.

---
