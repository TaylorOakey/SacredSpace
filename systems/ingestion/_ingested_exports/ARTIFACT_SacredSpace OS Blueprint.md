## 1. High-Level Map of SACREDSPACE OS

Think of the OS as a **city with 4 districts**:

1. **Presence Realm** – where you *live your day*  
2. **Memory Realm** – where everything you care about is *stored and woven together*  
3. **Action Realm** – where intentions become *tasks, events, and automations*  
4. **Lore Realm** – where your myth, characters, sigils, and worldbuilding *canon* lives

And then there are **two invisible layers** running underneath:

- **Data Flow Layer** – how stuff moves: phone → Obsidian → Drive → NotebookLM → Gemini  
- **Ritual Layer** – recurring check-ins, prompts, and ceremonies that keep it alive

**Primary tools:**
- **Obsidian** (on phone + laptop) – main interface
- **Android home screen** / widgets / quick capture
- A few pinned Google Docs/Sheets if needed

**Core structures in Presence:**

- In Obsidian, one root folder:
  - `SacredSpace OS/01 - Presence`
    - `Daily Logs/` – one note per day
    - `Rituals/` – morning, evening, weekly templates
    - `Now Board/` – “What matters *this week*”

- You open **today’s Daily Log** in Obsidian:

  - Bottom: any synchronicities/lore seeds (Lore Realm)
- Anything messy you capture goes into `Signals In/` and gets processed later into Memory/Lore.

Presence = “I’m here. This is what I’m doing today.”
