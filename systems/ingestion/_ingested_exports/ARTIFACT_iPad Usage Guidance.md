## 1. Start with a **Family Tech Covenant** (the foundation)
Before any apps, settings, or timers, anchor meaning.

Create a **simple spoken agreement** you say together when you first set them up:

> “This iPad is a tool for learning, creating, and kindness.  
> It helps me grow my mind, express my ideas, and take care of my world.  
> When I use it well, I earn trust. When I misuse it, I pause and reflect.”

This frames the device as **responsibility + privilege**, not entitlement.  
Kids *rise* to meaning when it’s named.

You can even print this and keep it near the charging station 🕯️

---

## 2. Use **Apple Screen Time** as Sacred Boundaries (non-negotiable)
Apple already gives you a monastery gate. You just need to bless it 😄

### Core Settings (per child Apple ID):
- **Daily Limit**:  
  - Weekdays: **60–90 minutes total**
  - Weekends: **90–120 minutes**
- **Downtime**:  
  - Example: 7:30pm – 7:00am (no exceptions)
- **Always Allowed**:
  - Reading apps
  - Drawing / music creation apps
  - Educational apps you approve

Everything else requires permission.

This turns *you* from “police” into **guardian of the threshold**.

---

## 3. Divide the iPad into **Three Sacred Modes**
Teach them this explicitly. Kids love modes.

### 🌱 Learn Mode
- Reading
- Math games
- Nature videos
- Language apps
- School-related use

Rule:  
**Learn Mode earns time**.

---

### 🎨 Create Mode
- Drawing
- Music
- Story writing
- Stop-motion
- Coding

Rule:  
**Create Mode is always celebrated** (even if messy).

---

### 🎮 Play Mode (limited, intentional)
- Games
- Entertainment

Rule:  
**Play Mode only happens after Learn or Create**.

This mirrors SacredSpace:  
**Presence → Creation → Play**, never the other way around.

---

## 4. Curate the Home Screen like a Garden
Do not let the App Store be the wild jungle.

You choose:
- Which apps exist
- Where they live
- Which folders they belong to

Example folders:
- 📚 Learn
- 🎨 Create
- 🌍 Explore
- 🎮 Play (small, last row)

The **visual layout teaches values** without lectures.

---

## 5. Introduce a **Daily Tech Ritual** (2 minutes)
Before they start:

1. Sit
2. One breath
3. Ask:
   - “What am I here to learn or make today?”

When time is up:
- Put iPad back in its **home/charging altar**
- No sneaking to bedrooms

This trains **conscious entry and exit**—a life skill, not just a rule.

---

## 6. Weekly Reflection (5 minutes, together)
Once a week:
- “What did you learn?”
- “What did you create?”
- “What do you want to try next?”

No punishment.  
No guilt.  
Just reflection.

This turns tech from consumption into **story**.

---

## 7. Long-Term: iPads as Living Portfolios
Over time:
- Save drawings
- Save stories
- Save music
- Save photos of real-world projects

Their iPads become:
> “A record of who I am becoming.”

That’s SacredSpace in digital form.
