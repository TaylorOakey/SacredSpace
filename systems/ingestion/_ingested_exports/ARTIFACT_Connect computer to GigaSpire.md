- *SSID: GigaSpire-XXXX*

# ⭐ Step 1 — Confirm You’re Plugged Into the Correct Port

On the **GigaSpire**, the Ethernet ports matter:

- **WAN** = where the internet *comes in*  
- **LAN 1 / LAN 2 / LAN 3 / LAN 4** = where devices (your computer) plug in

For your computer, the cable must be in a **LAN** port, *not* WAN.

**Check the back:**
- If you’re in a port labeled **LAN**, perfect.  
- If you accidentally plugged into **WAN**, move it to a LAN.
