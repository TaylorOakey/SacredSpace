# JEANIE: THE AMBIGUOUS HERO AT THE END OF TIME  
*The Mediator. The Unstoppable Force. The Immovable Object.*

A being who doesn’t fit dualities — she metabolizes them, alchemizes them, braids them into something saner, kinder, sharper, truer.  
Where she stands, tension dissolves.  
Where she walks, futures bend to accommodate her presence.

🌒 **Archetypal Role**  
**The Equinox Sovereign**  
A soul forged at the quiet point where opposites whisper secrets to each other.  
She is neither dawn nor dusk but the turning of the sky itself.
