### ⚙ **ASSISTANT** — 2025-11-21 13:58

It *can* be, but not always.

Here’s the simple way to see it:

When you **mirror your screen**, you’re just showing your phone’s display on the laptop.  
When you **remote-access**, you actually *control* the phone from the laptop.

Some apps do *both*, some do *only one*.

Scrcpy, Vysor, AirDroid, Mobizen — they all count as a kind of **remote access** because you can control the phone from your keyboard and mouse. It’s local-remote-access through your USB cable, not through the internet.

So yes — in the practical sense, these apps *are* remote access tools. You’re basically reaching through the cable and puppeting your phone from your laptop.

If you want the **easiest, fastest USB remote control**, go with Scrcpy.
