## What to check / watch out for

- **Rust and corrosion**: Many parts look good but metal corrosion inside (like on sensors) can make them fail fast.  
- **Compatibility**: Make sure engine size and drivetrain type match yours (4-cyl / V6 / FWD / manual vs auto).  
- **Wear & Tear**: Suspensions parts are often worn; check bushings, welds, etc.  
- **Cracks / Damage**: On plastic trim, lenses, etc. These degrade resale value or usefulness.  
- **Electronics:** Even if they look good, test if possible (lights, motors, switches).
