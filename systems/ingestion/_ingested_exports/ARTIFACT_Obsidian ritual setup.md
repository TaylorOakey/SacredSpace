## The Truth First (no illusion spells 🧠)
On mobile:
- ❌ You **cannot assign custom keyboard hotkeys** like Alt + E
- ✅ You **can still summon templates instantly**, just through gestures and UI anchors

Think of mobile as **gesture magic**, not key magic.

---

## The Mobile Equivalent of “Insert Template”

### 🔹 Method 1: Command Palette (Best & Built-In)
This is your primary mobile summon.

1. Open any note
2. Tap the **⋮ (three dots)** in the top-right
3. Tap **Command palette**
4. Choose **Templater: Insert Template**
5. Select your template

📜 This is the mobile version of chanting the spell name aloud.

Now:
- Open Command Palette  
- Tap the starred command at the top  
- Template appears

⚡ Two taps = manifestation.
