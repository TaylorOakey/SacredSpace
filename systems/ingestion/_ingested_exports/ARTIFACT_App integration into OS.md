## 1. What we’re stealing from each app

### From LevelUp (keep it SIMPLE)
We pull:

1. **Low-friction logging**
   - One action = one tap.
   - No 47-step setup.
   - “I did a 10-minute sweep of the living room” → log, done.

2. **Physical-effort rewards**
   - It shines when it treats activity like “reps” or “rounds.”
   - Perfect for:
     - 1 Cleanup Sprint = 10–15 minutes of focused cleaning.
     - Count sprints, not perfection.

3. **Streaks & momentum**
   - Simple streaks: “Days I did *some* cleanup.”
   - No shame if you miss a day; just reset and keep going.

### From Habitica (keep the FUN, tame the CHAOS)
We pull:

1. **Task Types = Game Objects**
   - **Habits** → micro-moves: “Put one thing away,” “Wipe one surface.”
   - **Dailies** → anchor rituals: “Morning reset,” “Evening sweep.”
   - **To-Dos** → boss fights: “Deep clean fridge,” “Tame the toy volcano.”

2. **Avatar & Gear**
   - You (and family) have characters that grow as the house grows cleaner.
   - Progress is visible and fun, not just “less dust.”

3. **Custom Rewards**
   - Use in-game gold to “buy”:
     - Solo walk
     - 20 minutes of music
     - Cozy show with kids
   - Rewards = soul food, not junk.

4. **Party / Family Mode**
   - Everyone can be in a party.
   - Shared “quests” for big cleans.
   - The house becomes a co-op dungeon, not a solo punishment.

We IGNORE for now:
- Overly complex navigation
- Min-maxing stats
- Giant walls of features

We just use the skeleton.

## 2. SacredSpace Cleanup Game v2: Core Design

Think of this as a **module** in your SacredSpace OS:  
**CITY & STRUCTURE → Home Realms → Cleanup Game**

### Core concepts

- **Realms** = rooms / spaces  
  - Kitchen Realm  
  - Living Room Realm  
  - Bathroom Realm  
  - Kids’ Rooms, etc.

- **Zones** = specific slices of a Realm  
  - “Kitchen • Sink Zone”  
  - “Living Room • Couch Zone”

- **Quests** = specific cleanup actions  
  - “Clear the Sink Zone”  
  - “Toy Rescue Mission in Couch Zone”

- **Rituals** = repeating patterns  
  - Morning Reset  
  - Evening Reset  
  - Pre-bedroom sweep with the kids

- **Bosses** = big, sometimes scary tasks  
  - “Laundry Mountain”  
  - “Fridge of Forgotten Realms”

- **Loot / XP** = points you cash in for meaningful rewards

---

## 3. How it plays (simple loop)

**Step 1: Choose a Realm**  
“What realm needs love right now?” → Kitchen, Living Room, etc.

**Step 2: Pick ONE Quest (or Ritual)**  
Keep it tiny:
- “10-minute sweep of floor”
- “Clear all surfaces in Sink Zone”
- “Put away 15 items in kids’ room”

**Step 3: Run a Cleanup Sprint**
- Set timer: 10–15 minutes.
- Focus only on that micro-quest. No bouncing around.

**Step 4: Log XP in app**
- 1 Sprint = e.g. **10 XP**
- Boss battle might = 30–50 XP

**Step 5: Claim or save rewards**
- 20 XP = music & dance break  
- 50 XP = creative time  
- 100 XP = date night / longer ritual

Do this a few times a day, not for hours. This keeps it playful.

## 4. Wiring this into Habitica (practical version)

Open Habitica and set it up like this:

### A. Tags (organize by Realms)
Create tags:
- `Kitchen Realm`
- `Living Room Realm`
- `Bathroom Realm`
- `Kids' Realm`
- `Laundry Realm`

You’ll attach these to your tasks so you can filter by “Where am I right now?”

### B. Habits (micro-moves)

Add 3–7 Habits like:

- **“Put one thing in its home”**  
  - Tag: All Realms  
  - Use when you pass through any room and put *one* thing away.

- **“Wipe one surface”**  
  - Tag: Kitchen, Bathroom, Living Room

- **“Empty one small container / basket”**  
  - Tag: Laundry, Kids’ Realm

- **“Throw away 3 obvious trash items”**  
  - Tag: All Realms

These are your “I only have 90 seconds” buttons.

### C. Dailies (anchor rituals)

Make a few Dailies:

- **“Morning Reset: Kitchen & Living Room”**  
  Checklist:
  - Clear counters  
  - Dishes in sink or washer  
  - Trash consolidated

- **“Evening Reset: Pathways & Surfaces”**  
  Checklist:
  - Clear paths (no tripping hazards)  
  - Wipe main table  
  - Quick sweep or vacuum

- **“Kids’ Room Reset (5-minute race)”**  
  - All toys off floor  
  - Dirty clothes in hamper  
  - Books roughly stacked

Set Dailies to repeat on the days that feel realistic, not all seven if that will stress you.

### D. To-Dos (boss fights)

These are one-offs. Example To-Dos:

- “Deep clean fridge (Fridge Boss I)”
- “Sort one laundry basket mountain”
- “Purge 10 items from closet”
- “Clean car interior (Mobile Realm)”

Each To-Do can have checklists inside (e.g. shelves, drawers, etc.).
