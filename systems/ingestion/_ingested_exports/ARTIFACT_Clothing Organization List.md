## **SACREDSPACE: Clothing Organization Flow 🧺✨**

### **1. Empty the Closet (The Great Clearing)**
- Remove *all* random items from the closet  
- Place them neatly in the storage spot under the bed  
- Quick sweep of the empty closet: dust, wipe, refresh the air  

---

### **2. Form the Four Piles (The Sorting Ritual)**
Create one pile for each person:
- ∆ You  
- Jeannie  
- Asher  
- Iris  

*(Big heap becomes four mini-mountains.)*

---

### **3. Choose Two at a Time (Dual Sorting Method)**
For each pair:
- Bring both individual piles to the sorting zone  
- Quick scan together for:
  - Keep
  - Give away
  - Needs washing
  - Trash / unusable  

---

### **4. Handle the KEEP Items (Return them to the Realm)**
- Hang up all shirts, jackets, dresses  
- Fold pants, shorts, pajamas  
- Assign each person a sacred zone in the closet  
- Align colors if desired  
- Place seasonal or bulky pieces higher or lower  

---

### **5. Handle the GIVE AWAY Items (Passing Them On)**
- Place in a dedicated donation bag or box  
- Label: “Giveaway – Clothing”  
- Store near the door or car for easy drop-off  

---

### **6. Handle the WASH Items**
- Put directly into laundry hamper  
- If stain-treating needed, set aside a small “Alchemy” bowl  

---

### **7. Handle TRASH / UNUSABLE Items**
- Bag these separately  
- Remove from the room immediately to prevent pile-gravity  

---

### **8. Final Touch (Bless the Space)**
- Quick sweep or vacuum  
- Place one intentional object on a shelf (crystal, photo, tiny plant)  
- Step back, admire the order you’ve summoned
