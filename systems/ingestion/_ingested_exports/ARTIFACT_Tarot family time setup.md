# 🃏 Tarot Setup Guide: Family, Discipline & Creativity  

## 1. The Question  
*"How can I spend more quality time with my family in a way that builds self-discipline and encourages creativity?"*

---

## 2. Preparing the Space  
- **Choose a calm spot** where everyone feels comfortable (living room floor, kitchen table, porch, etc.).  
- **Set the mood**: light a candle, burn incense, or play soft instrumental music.  
- **Sacred Centerpiece**: place one object in the center that represents your family bond (a stone, plant, photo, or meaningful object).  

---

## 3. Grounding Ritual (2–3 minutes)  
- Everyone takes a deep breath in and out together.  
- Hold hands or touch the shared object.  
- Say aloud: *“We invite guidance that helps us grow in love, discipline, and creativity.”*  

---

## 4. The Spread (5 Cards)  

Lay the cards in a **circle or star pattern** to symbolize balance:  

1. **The Heart of Family Time** – What defines our togetherness right now?  
2. **The Path of Self-Discipline** – How do we create healthy structure and accountability?  
3. **The Spark of Creativity** – How do we keep our imagination alive together?  
4. **Challenges or Distractions** – What might pull us away from this vision?  
5. **The Sacred Practice** – What regular ritual can unify us in both creativity and discipline?  

---

## 5. Reflection Process  
- After each card is revealed, let everyone share what the image or meaning reminds them of.  
- Write down one key word or phrase for each card on a family journal page.  
- At the end, together decide on **one action or ritual** to try that week (example: a nightly drawing session, a shared chore with music, or a storytelling walk in nature).  

---

## 6. Closing  
- Thank the deck (and each other).  
- End with everyone saying a word that represents what they want to bring into family time this week (e.g., “patience,” “play,” “focus,” “joy”).
